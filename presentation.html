Problem Statement vs Solution

Why Universities Need CampusPulse
❌ The Problem
  • Scattered Notices: Posters lost in noisy WhatsApp chats and bulletin boards.
  • Gate Overcrowding: Paper sign-in sheets create slow entry queues and proxy logins.
  • Delayed Certificates: Students wait months to receive participation certificates.
  • Expired Feed Clutter: Past events clutter active notice boards.
✅ The CampusPulse Solution
  • Unified AI Feed: Real-time discovery with category & department filters.
  • QR Venue Check-In: Scannable entry passes for 1-second gate verification.
  • Instant PDF Certificates: Verified certificates unlocked in student wallets.
  • Auto Expiry Archiving: Ended events automatically move to searchable archives.
